The regional average gives equal weight to each distinct selected site pixel. It represents these named sites; the selection has not been clipped to an ONMS boundary. Monthly values require every calendar day, and the regional mean requires all selected unique pixels. Missing or partial months appear as gaps. Map cells show 0.05° × 0.05° pixel footprints (approximately 5 km). Purple outlines show NOAA ONMS boundaries for geographic context; they do not change site inclusion or averages.
