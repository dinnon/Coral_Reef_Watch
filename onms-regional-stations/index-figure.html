SST is the monthly mean of NOAA's daily value at the pixel selected by the regional 90th-percentile HotSpot; that pixel can change daily. The DHW view shows the month's highest daily station DHW, with a 4 °C-weeks reference for NOAA Bleaching Alert Level 1. That alert also requires a HotSpot of at least 1°C on the same day; crossing the line alone does not establish bleaching. Incomplete months are excluded.
