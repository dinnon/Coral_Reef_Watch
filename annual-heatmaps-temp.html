Data and averaging method

These figures reuse the saved annual sanctuary-average CSVs from the ONMS annual map atlas. NOAA supplies the annual mean SST and annual mean SST anomaly grids. No new anomaly baseline or temporal averages are calculated here.

Ocean pixels are included when their footprints overlap the saved ONMS boundary by a positive area. Each value is weighted by its overlap area on the WGS84 ellipsoid; partial pixels contribute only their overlapping area. Land is excluded. Missing/ice/fill cells do not contribute to the valid-area mean. Coverage is valid overlap area divided by selected non-land overlap area for that year, not the full sanctuary polygon.

These are satellite ocean-area averages, including offshore waters—not reef-only averages, regional virtual stations, or observed bleaching assessments. Each panel uses one fixed color scale across all regions and years; changing the displayed years does not rescale colors. Missing data are hatched gray. No uncertainty interval is inferred.

Source: NOAA Coral Reef Watch v3.1 annual products. The saved snapshot is 1985–2025; this file works offline and does not refresh automatically.
